How to play

Everyone gets the same 16 letters each day. Drag them onto the board and build one connected crossword — every row and column of touching letters must be a real word.

Words that aren't words glow red — rearrange tiles as much as you like. The clock starts on your first tile and stops when all 16 are down and everything checks out. That's a TAP 🍁

Fastest taps make the monthly leaderboard. New letters every midnight (Vermont time, obviously).
